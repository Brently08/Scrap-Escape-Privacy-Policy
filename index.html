Privacy Policy

Scrap Escape is a puzzle game in which little robots escape a scrapyard. This policy explains what the game stores and what it does not. It applies to Scrap Escape on Android and iOS.

1. Who the game is for

Scrap Escape is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us (below) and we will help you remove the data from the device.

2. What the game stores

Everything is stored only on your own device. We run no server for the game and keep no copy of your data. This includes:

  • Your player nickname and a scrambled (hashed) password, if you create a player profile. You can also play as a guest.
  • Your progress, stars, scores, Nuts & Bolts balance and Wardrobe choices.
  • Levels you build and save in the level editor.
  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, large text and similar).

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not include chat or messaging between players.

3. Advertising

Scrap Escape may show ads, including optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for bonus Nuts & Bolts, through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and use certain device information, such as your advertising ID, IP address and general device details, under Google's advertising policies. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your phone's settings. Some test versions of the game show no ads at all.

4. Purchases

If the game offers a one-time purchase to remove ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

5. Analytics and tracking

The game does not include its own analytics or tracking tools. The only third-party service is the advertising service described above. If we add anything else, we will update this page first.

6. Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. Keep your device software up to date.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new date.

Delete your account and data

Your account, progress and saved levels exist only on your device, so there is nothing stored with us to delete. You can erase everything yourself in any of these ways:

In the game: open Settings and tap Delete my account (for named players). This permanently deletes the account and its saved progress. Reset my progress wipes progress for the current player.

Uninstall the game: press and hold the Scrap Escape icon and choose Uninstall (Android) or Remove App → Delete App (iPhone/iPad). This removes all game data from the device.

Android, keep the app installed: Settings → Apps → Scrap Escape → Storage → Clear data.

Deleted data cannot be recovered, because no backup exists. Ad data handled by Google can be managed in your device's advertising settings.
